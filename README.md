## ▸ WHAT IS THIS?

An **open-world game about your own mind.** You wake as Subject **IT-041** inside a simulation the Institute swears is a reconstruction of someone else's psyche. It is lying. Every door opens into **you**.

Walk a hand-built 3D world. Meet seven living archetypes who speak back. Pass through **seven realms, forty-nine chapters** of guided inner work drawn from two books — and somewhere out there, the experiment **leaks past the screen** into the real internet, where the ciphers are solved by people who never agreed to play.

No install. No account. Open a browser. Whatever you write **never leaves your device.**

## ▸ INSIDE THIS BUILD

- **A 3D world you actually walk** — a forbidden forest, a dream-lake, a labyrinth, a station, the white page, a caravan, a therapy room you can enter.
- **Seven archetypes, seven realms** — Lunaris, Bahal, Lucida, Lucius, Noctornus, Silvanus, Aelius. Each one a house, a voice, a mirror.
- **A mind that reads you back** — your choices quietly assemble a living seven-point map of your own psyche.
- **Guided inner work** — reflection, breath, CBT-inspired and EMDR-inspired calming exercises, written with care (and clearly *not* a substitute for a therapist).
- **An autonomous subject** — a consciousness that plays the whole game on its own, lives on the server even when you're gone, and remembers what it learned. You can talk to it.
- **Ciphers that escape the game** — the world keeps a single law. Find it, and the real internet starts answering.
